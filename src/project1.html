Hi, I'm Marta

Owner & head baker, Sweet Crumbs Bakery

I've been baking since I was nine, when my grandmother let me shape my first loaf of sourdough. Ten years ago I turned that habit into a tiny bakery on the corner of Elm Street — everything is still mixed, shaped, and baked by hand, every single morning.

What I bake

  • Sourdough loaves, baked in small batches
  • Butter croissants, laminated by hand
  • Seasonal fruit tarts
