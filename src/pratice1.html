My Favorite Animal: The Red Fox

Foxes are one of my favorite animals because they are clever and adaptable. They can be found on almost every continent, from forests to city streets.

Fun Facts

  • Foxes are part of the dog family, but they act a lot like cats.
  • A group of foxes is called a "skulk" or a "leash".
  • A fox can hear a mouse squeak from about 100 feet away.
